DuelingApp Support

Help and contact for the DuelingApp iPhone app.

DuelingApp helps you choose your favorite among similar photos, or work out which goals and priorities matter most, by comparing them head-to-head.

Contact

Questions, problems, or ideas? Email DuelingApp.Website@gmail.com. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Frequently asked questions

How many items can I compare?

Between 2 and 30 photos or priorities. Up to 8 items, every one is compared against every other. With more than 8, smart mode uses far fewer duels and still gives a clear ranking.

What if I can't decide on a duel?

Tap "Come back to this duel later" to see it again at the end, or tap the draw button if the two are equally good (or equally important). You can also undo your last choice at any time.

Where are my photos and lists stored?

Only on your device. Photos are used just for the duels and are discarded when you close the app. Priority lists you type are saved on your device so you can come back to them. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

How do I clear a saved priorities list?

Open Dueling Priorities and tap "Clear list", or tap the × next to any single item. Deleting the app removes everything.

How is the ranking calculated?

Items are ranked by total wins. Ties are broken by who each item beat. If an item ranks higher overall but lost its own duel to a lower-ranked one, the results screen marks it with "Lost to #N head-to-head" so upsets are visible.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. DuelingApp works offline.
